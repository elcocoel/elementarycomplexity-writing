## The full story

Still here? Then you want the hero's journey.

### Before the series: when the DAO space was full of dreams

**Introduction to DAOs.** I fell for DAOs early, when the whole space was full of dreams, me included. Genesis DAO made me the first person to pay all my bills through proposals to a DAO, and this was my first talk about it.

::talk{url="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sCxb4JkrVGw" meta="Introduction to DAOs · my first talk" start="300" title="Introduction to DAOs"}

**DAO Design Canvas · Devcon 5, 2019.** The first thing I gave back was a map: a workshop where the room designed two DAOs from scratch, then took the canvas itself apart and told me how to improve it.

::talk{url="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U5i-R52plLE" meta="DAO Design Canvas · Devcon 5, Osaka · 2019" start="60" title="DAO Design Canvas"}

**DAOfest · ETHDenver, 2020.** How a network of local organisers could coordinate events, pay each other and own what they built together. DAOfest ran more than thirty meetups across four continents in three months.

::talk{url="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cRxV7Iq4S-M" meta="DAOfest · ETHDenver · 2020" start="60" title="DAOfest"}

### The series: identity, relationship and shared success narratives

**1 · Collaborative Defiance · Lisbon, 2021.** The opening statement of The DAOist, at Village Underground: why a group of DAO builders should gather at all, and what we were refusing together.

::talk{url="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B1YhZLmQi5M" meta="Collaborative Defiance · Lisbon · 2021 · rough sound, good talk" start="90" title="Collaborative Defiance"}

**2 · A Life Well Spent · Governauts, 2021.** Reward systems quietly define what success looks like inside a community, which makes designing them one of the most political acts there is. This is where I first named the mother polarity that runs through the rest of the series: the more a group manifests identity, the more relationships it closes off, and the more it opens to relationship, the less its identity holds.

::talk{url="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gckg5w01fLE" meta="A Life Well Spent · Governauts · 2021 · 31 min" start="120" title="A Life Well Spent"}

**3 · Of Moats and Clouds · ETHDenver, 2022.** You can't own a cloud, and you don't own a community. Narratives are public goods, and whoever stands at the centre of attention is only temporarily blessed to steward them. The work is to design attractors that keep people ignited, find language for winning together, and hand the story on.

::talk{url="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ib1SbYrH1ag" meta="Of Moats and Clouds · ETHDenver · 2022 · 15 min" start="60" title="Of Moats and Clouds"}

**4 · The DAOist as a Global Connective · Barcelona, 2022.** A connective rather than a collective: a place where groups come to sense, disagree constructively and go back to their own work stronger. Flow over control, relationships over structure, process over product.

::talk{url="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cka-QABDuZk" meta="The DAOist as a Global Connective · Barcelona · 2022" start="120" title="The DAOist as a Global Connective"}

**5 · Awareness of the Continuum · ETHBarcelona, 2022.** The practitioner's chapter, for the people mopping up emotional damage inside DAOs: when to pulse and when to flow, perception moves before resolution moves, and why the solution has to come from the system itself.

::talk{url="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XKML9_EL-1o" meta="Awareness of the Continuum · ETHBarcelona · 2022" start="90" title="Awareness of the Continuum"}

**6 · North Stars, unfinished.** The next chapter was about how founders and communities hold on to mission and principle when conditions turn sour. It existed as a talk proposal and a draft essay, Solving for Layer 0. Then my own conditions turned sour, and the series stopped there. [Rigorously Joyful](/writing/rigorously-joyful) is where I picked the thread back up.
